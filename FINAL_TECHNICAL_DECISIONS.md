# FINAL_TECHNICAL_DECISIONS.md — Single Source of Truth & Architectural Reconciliation Ledger

## 1. Purpose
During our multi-stage research, an initial rapid prototype (v1.0) was built and then subjected to a deep, critical reassessment (v2.0). This document resolves every discrepancy between earlier drafts and locks in the **single authoritative engineering specification** that Antigravity must implement.

## 2. Definitive Reconciliation Table (v1.0 Prototype vs. Final v2.0 Specification)

| Subsystem | Deprecated v1.0 Prototype Approach (DO NOT USE) | Final Approved v2.0 Architecture (MUST IMPLEMENT) | Technical Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Entity & Relationship Schema** | Flat `entities` and `relationships` tables with a single `confidence` float and overwritten `attributes_json`. | **[FollowTheMoney](https://github.com/alephdata/followthemoney)-aligned `entities` + Bitemporal Reified `claims` (`valid_from`, `valid_until`, `observed_at`) + 5-State `epistemic_status`**. | Preserves historical DNS/ownership changes over time and allows competing sources to assert conflicting values on the same entity without overwriting data. |
| **2. | **3. Entity Resolution Candidate Blocking** | All-pairs $O(N^2)$ nested loop over workspace entities. | **3-Pass Blocking (`entity_blocking_keys`): Sorted Neighborhood ($w=15$) + Exact Identifier Prefix + 3-Gram MinHash LSH**. | Reduces candidate pairs by **71.7×** and speeds up blocking by **47.5×** at $N=2,000$ (scales to 100,000+ entities on CPU). |
| **4. | **5.
